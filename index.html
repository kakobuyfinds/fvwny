From a spreadsheet row to an order

  1. Open the CAFANS detail page and confirm the variant, current price and availability. Save your chosen size or color alongside the product link in your exported shortlist.
  2. Check the shopping-agent options on the product page. If you use the original marketplace URL, confirm the item and variant in KakoBuy before placing the order.
  3. If warehouse QC photos are available, check the color, labels and measurements against your selected variant while there is still time to ask about a return or exchange.
  4. Get a shipping quote using the packed dimensions and destination. Check what the quote includes before adding taxes, duties or service fees to your budget.

KakoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a KakoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a browsable list of product listings that shoppers can compare before choosing an agent. This independent guide uses CAFANS listings and links each entry to its CAFANS detail page.

Does the displayed price include shipping?

The prices in the catalog are item estimates. Check the current seller price and use your agent's domestic delivery, fee and international shipping quotes to estimate the full cost.

Where can I check sizes and QC photos?

Check the seller listing for a size chart. For items received at a warehouse, check the QC photos and request measurements through your shopping agent if that service is available.

Can I use the shortlist in Google Sheets or Excel?

Save the products you want, download the CSV, and import it into your spreadsheet app. The export includes product names, categories, reference USD prices and CAFANS links.
